Engineering Meets Innovation

I'm Amir Hosin Sekhavatfar, an Electrical Engineering student with a deep passion for industrial automation and embedded systems. My journey began with curiosity about how machines think and communicate — leading me into the world of PLCs, SCADA systems, and modern IoT architectures.
